# Sources for explanation-writing

Checked on their pages on 2026-11-11. Diátaxis states no open licence,
so it is paraphrased here, never quoted.

- Diátaxis, [explanation](https://diataxis.fr/explanation/): explanation
  discusses a subject at length so the reader understands it, away from
  the task at hand. Its principles: make connections,
  provide background and context (design decisions, history, technical
  limits), talk about the subject so a title fits after an implicit
  "about", admit opinion and perspective and weigh alternatives, and
  keep explanation bounded so instruction and description stay in their
  own places.
- W3C WAI, [complex images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/):
  a complex image needs "a textual representation of the essential
  information conveyed by the image", hence a sentence after each
  diagram.
