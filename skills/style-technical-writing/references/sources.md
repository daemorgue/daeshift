# Sources for style-technical-writing

- The rules come from tired-labs/governance's Technical Writing style
  guide, a private TIRED Labs repository maintained by daemorgue, who
  approved its use here; references/rules.md copies three of its
  sections unchanged. The guide (version 1.0.0,
  style-guide/technical-writing.md) follows governance's Style Guide
  specification. Its purpose: prose a reader
  takes in only one way, written as from an impersonal source.
- The AI-tell word list comes from dotclaude's ai-prose-hygiene
  knowledge file; governance's punctuation rules already cover its
  punctuation rules (no em dashes, no exclamation marks).
- This style serves reference pages and specifications. Explanation,
  how-to guides and tutorials use style-user-documentation, because
  Diátaxis's explanation admits opinion and weighs alternatives, which
  this style forbids (decision-16 on the daemorgue board).
- The other side, checked 2026-11-11: Google's developer documentation
  style guide (https://developers.google.com/style/person) advises "Use
  the second person to address what the reader does", where this style
  names the party in the third person. This style keeps the third
  person for reference and specifications.
